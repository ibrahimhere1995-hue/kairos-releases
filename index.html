Everything in one calm place

Plan less. Do more of what matters.

Tasks, events, reminders and habits live together, so your day makes sense at a glance.

My Day

Open Kairos and see what's happening now, what's left today and how your week is balanced.

Calendar & time blocks

Day, week, month and agenda views. Drag a task onto the calendar to give it a time.

Reminders that reach you

Real notifications, even when the window is closed. Tick it off or snooze it right from the reminder.

Quick capture

Type "Call bank tomorrow 3pm #home" and Kairos understands the date, time and area. No forms.

Habits, goals & focus

Gentle streaks, goals with milestones, a focus timer, and a weekly review of how time was spent.

Smart extras, if you want them

Turn a screenshot into a task or plan your day with AI. Off by default; uses your own free Gemini key.

Capture in a second

Say it the way you think it.

Press Ctrl Shift Space anywhere, type a sentence, and press Enter. Kairos picks out the day, the time and the life area, and shows you what it understood before saving.

  • Works offline. The understanding happens on your computer.
  • Speak instead. On Windows, press Win H in any box and talk.

Kind by design

Missed something? It slipped, you didn't fail.

Kairos never shouts in red. Slipped tasks are shown in soft amber with one-click choices: today, tomorrow, pick a date, or let it go. If something keeps moving, Kairos suggests breaking it into smaller steps.

The privacy promise

Your planner lives with you.

Your plans are personal. Kairos keeps them on your computer, not on someone else's server.

No account

Works offline

Everything works without the internet, except the optional smart extras.

No tracking

No analytics, no ads, no data selling. This website doesn't track you either.

Automatic backups

Daily backup copies, one-click restore, and export to standard calendar files.
